Headset setup

iPhone: Safari can't go full screen, so use Share → Add to Home Screen and start the game from that icon.

In the test view the red cross should look single and sharp and the grid lines straight. Two crosses → adjust Lens distance or Screen size. Lines bowing outward or inward → adjust Distortion. Tap the screen (or headset button) to come back.

Walking & sensors
Walk in place holding the phone – the bar should jump with each step.

Every step you take – walking in place works – moves you forward in the direction you're looking, and you stop when you stop. The bar jumps with each step; each time it crosses the red line counts as one. Raise sensitivity if steps are missed, lower it if you move while standing still.

Graphics

Use Low if the picture stutters inside the headset – a smooth frame rate matters more than detail in VR. Resolution also lowers itself automatically if the phone can't keep up.
